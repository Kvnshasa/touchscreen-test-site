Day 1

What I'm building:
A website that allows users to do a touchscreen test and determine if their touchscreen
is faulty and needs repair or if it is working fine for touch-points and multi-touch.

Why I'm building:
Allows me to learn web development skills, build something in real time and to learn
SEO skills in optimising the website to be near the top of results page to get traffic
and thereby generate revenue via Google Adsense.

What I want to achieve in 30 days:
I want to have a functioning website that either generates me a small but sizeable amount of
income at the end of each month or atleast have palpable traffic that shows that this is worth
continuing and that I'm certainly making progress with this, rather than wasting my time.

Metrics I will track:
- Website visitors
- Touchscreen test uses
- Google search clicks 
- Google search impressions
- Average search position
- Revenue (At the end of the 30 days)

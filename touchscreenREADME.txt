HOW TO OPEN
Open index.html in a web browser.

WHAT WORKS
- Start Test button
- Reset Test button
- Single touch points
- Multiple simultaneous touch points
- Active touch counter
- Maximum simultaneous touch counter
- Touch points follow the pointer
- Touch points are removed when the pointer is released/cancelled/leaves

BIGGEST BUGS FIXED
1. Touch points could become stuck after an interrupted interaction.
2. Removing a touch point sometimes caused an "undefined" error.
3. Touch points could move outside the test area.

What is a touchscreen test?

A test to check whether or not your device's screen is responsive to touch

How to test a phone touchscreen?

We offer a free touchscreen test tool
1. Click on Start to begin
2. Move your finger across the 5 regions
3. A result will be shown at the bottom
4. If it displays that all 5 regions have been touched, you've tested those main regions for dead zones
5. Click Reset to restart the test

What to look for

Areas which seem to not respond to being touched or pressed. These are 'dead zones' which are areas of the screen where the touchscreen is unresponsive and may indicate a hardware issue.

Common touchscreen problems

Dead zones

Parts of the screen don't respond to touch.

Missed touches

You touch the screen but nothing happens.

Ghost touches

The screen registers touches that you didn't make. Some typical causes are by a wet screen surface, software glitches or a damaged digitizer.If noticed, there may be an issue with your touchscreen which could require repair.

Multi-touch problems

The screen doesn't properly detect multiple fingers.

Unresponsive areas

Certain regions consistently fail to register touch.

What does it mean if one region doesn't respond?

If one region doesn't respond to touch, it may indicate that there is an unresponsive area or dead zone on the screen. Try touching the same area again to make sure it wasn't a missed touch. If the area consistently doesn't respond, there may be an issue with the touchscreen.

Limitations of touchscreen testing

It's impossible to precisely check whether every area of the screen is working. The majority of the screen may be working, while very small sections may be unresponsive and hard to detect. Our touchscreen test offers a useful indication as to whether or not your touchscreen is working but it cannot give a precise answer if your touchscreen is completely working or not.

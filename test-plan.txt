Visitor Flow:
- Land on page
- Read instructions
- Click button to start the test
- Touch and see whether or not points were detected
- Stop/Release
- See result after a few seconds/minute

Single-Touch Behaviour:
One visible point which follows the user around as they click/move their mouse
or finger around the test area.

Multi-Touch Behaviour:
Severala visivle points that follow the user around as they click/mouse their mouse
or fingers on multiple areas in the test area. (atleast 2)

Useful results:
- Maximum simultaneous touches
- Whether or not the test area responded
- If movement was detected in multiple regions
- Final verdict if their touchscreen appears to be working or not

Limitation:
- Cannot tell exactly if every single part of the screen is working

Touchscreen Test

About

This tool is a touchscreen tester allowing anyone to test their screen for dead zones and multi-touch. Suitable for anyone who wants to test their laptop, phone, tablet or any touchscreen device. This test takes only a few seconds to do.

How to use it

1. Click on Start to begin
2. Move your finger across the 5 regions
3. A result will be shown at the bottom
4. If it displays that all 5 regions have been touched, you've tested the full screen for dead zones
5. Click Reset to restart the test

What is multi-touch?

The screen's ability to recognise more than one point of contact. This touchscreen test allows multiple fingers to be tested and multiple corresponding dots should appear to match those fingers.

What does the result mean?

When you test with more than one finger, the active touch count should rise above 1 - that's how you know multi-touch is working. The regions counter shows how much of the screen you've actually covered; reaching 5 of 5 means you've tested every corner and the center, which helps you spot a dead zone if one area never seems to register.
